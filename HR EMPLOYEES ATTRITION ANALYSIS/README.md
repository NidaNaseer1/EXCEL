# Employee Attrition Analysis

## Project Overview
The **Employee Attrition Analysis** project focuses on analyzing employee turnover using HR data. The main objective is to understand employee attrition patterns and identify the factors that influence employees to leave an organization.

An interactive HR Attrition Dashboard is developed to visualize employee data and provide meaningful insights into attrition across different departments, job roles, age groups, gender, salary ranges, education fields, years of service, and business travel categories.

## Dashboard KPIs

The dashboard highlights the following key performance indicators:

| KPI | Value |
|---|---:|
| Total Employees | 1,470 |
| Attrition Count | 237 |
| Active Employees | 1,233 |
| Attrition Rate | 16.12% |
| Average Age | 36.92 |

## Dashboard Analysis

The dashboard provides the following visualizations:

1. **Employee Attrition by Department** – Analyzes employee attrition across Human Resources, Research & Development, and Sales.
2. **Employee Attrition by Gender** – Compares attrition between male and female employees.
3. **Employee Attrition by Job Role** – Identifies attrition patterns across different job positions.
4. **Employee Attrition by Age** – Examines employee attrition across different age groups.
5. **Employee Attrition by Salary** – Analyzes attrition based on salary ranges.
6. **Employee Attrition by Years** – Studies attrition based on years of service.
7. **Employee Attrition by Education Field** – Compares attrition across different educational backgrounds.
8. **Employee Attrition by Business Travel** – Analyzes the relationship between business travel frequency and employee attrition.

## Key Insights

- The organization has 1,470 employees, with 237 employees leaving the company.
- The overall attrition rate is 16.12%.
- A total of 1,233 employees are currently active.
- The average employee age is approximately 37 years.
- The dashboard helps compare attrition across departments, job roles, age groups, salary categories, and other HR factors.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze employee attrition patterns.
- Identify departments and job roles with higher attrition.
- Understand employee demographics.
- Examine salary and experience-related attrition patterns.
- Support HR teams in making data-driven decisions.

## Conclusion

This project provides an interactive overview of employee attrition and workforce demographics. The dashboard helps HR teams understand employee turnover patterns and identify areas that may require attention to improve employee retention.
